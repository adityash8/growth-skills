# Growth Roadmap

Use this skill after a constraint has been diagnosed. If the problem is still “we need growth,” run `growth-strategist` or `growth-audit` first.

## Inputs

- business outcome, baseline, and planning horizon;
- diagnosed constraint and supporting evidence;
- target audience or segment;
- current initiatives and prior experiment results;
- people, budget, engineering, legal, and timing constraints;
- dependencies and fixed commitments.

## Procedure

1. **Write one outcome.** Use a business result, not an activity. Include the baseline, direction, horizon, and measurement owner when known.
2. **Name the constraint.** Explain why it blocks the outcome and what evidence could disprove it.
3. **Choose one strategic bet.** State the mechanism that should move the constraint. Reject parallel themes that compete for the same scarce resource.
4. **Build a hypothesis set.** Limit the quarter to three primary hypotheses. Each must connect intervention → behavior → leading signal → business result.
5. **Design the portfolio.** Balance fast diagnostics, one or two compounding investments, and explicitly protected capacity. Avoid a queue of unrelated tactics.
6. **Prioritize honestly.** Score expected impact as a range, confidence from evidence, effort in person-time, and dependency risk. Do not multiply invented numbers for theatrical precision.
7. **Sequence dependencies.** Put instrumentation and enabling work before tests that rely on them. State what can run in parallel.
8. **Assign decisions.** Name the owner only when known. Define the review cadence and the threshold for scale, revise, or stop.
9. **Protect focus.** Create a stop list and a parking lot. A roadmap without exclusions is a backlog.

Use `references/roadmap-template.md` for the final artifact.

## Output contract

Return:

1. **Outcome and baseline.**
2. **Constraint and evidence.**
3. **Quarterly strategic bet.**
4. **Three primary hypotheses.**
5. **Sequenced experiment and enablement table.**
6. **Decision rules:** scale, revise, stop.
7. **Capacity and dependency map.**
8. **Stop list and parking lot.**
9. **Risks and evidence gaps.**

## Guardrails

- Do not build a roadmap before choosing the constraint.
- Do not use tasks as key results.
- Do not assign more work than available capacity.
- Do not hide dependencies inside experiment estimates.
- Do not call an initiative an experiment without a falsifiable prediction.
- Do not continue a test because effort has already been spent.
- Replan when the constraint changes; do not defend the calendar.
