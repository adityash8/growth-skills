# Stakeholder-facing paid analysis review

Use when a paid-acquisition narrative combines creative quality, setup choices, scaling, and attribution. Make each conclusion earn its place.

## Find the load-bearing claim

Write one sentence that the evidence actually supports. If the evidence concerns the creative-to-delivery handoff, show how the asset, objective, optimization event, allocation, and outcome connect. Do not assume that the handoff is the cause before checking it.

Use a short thesis, one relevant proof case, a source reconciliation, and an explicit operational ask. A proof case illustrates a failure mode; it does not establish prevalence across the account.

## Source checks

- Use ad-platform reads for spend, identity, objective, optimization event, bid settings, targeting, and delivery.
- Use product analytics for outcome health and cohort quality. All-channel volume is a magnitude check until an entity crosswalk exists.
- Use connector exports to check mapping, completeness, and freshness. A mirror of the platform is not independent customer truth.
- Use billing or warehouse records for the agreed paid-outcome and customer definitions, with model and identity limitations stated.

## Experiment and allocation checks

- Read every embedded chart alongside extracted text. Fail sign-off when the chart and narrative disagree on totals, windows, or population.
- Separate attributed event credit, unique acquired customers, and incremental outcomes.
- Platform learning guidance is not a statistical power calculation or a guarantee of stable CAC. Verify current guidance and set a decision-specific evidence gate.
- Sum concurrent allocations before calling consolidation budget-neutral.
- Equal-budget ad groups are not randomized arms without verified assignment and exposure.
- Allow conversion maturity before freezing the result; a fixed runtime alone does not establish adequacy.

## Replacement language

| Unsupported claim | Evidence-aware replacement |
|---|---|
| This format causes lower CAC | This format appears among scaled winners; delivery and audience differences remain confounded |
| The creator failed | This cell missed the agreed outcome gate under the observed setup |
| The example proves the whole category works | The example identifies a setup issue; category performance needs a clean test |
| Scale the small winner | Run a bounded scale test with a protected control and mature outcome gate |
| Engagement proves acquisition quality | Engagement is promising; downstream conversion and retention remain unverified |

State what the evidence supports without inventing person or team blame.

## Attribution wording

Keep tracker cost per acquisition, verified-customer cost per acquisition, and raw platform conversion discrepancies as separate comparisons. Each needs its own denominator, model, window, and source. State uncertainty instead of borrowing precision from a dashboard.

## Rendered-artifact QA

- Label dates, units, currencies, and population clearly.
- Distinguish abstract diagrams from measured account performance.
- Keep raw proof and long methods in the appendix when the executive artifact must stay short.
- Inspect exported charts for clipping and count/cost label collisions.
- Remove duplicate footers and stale graphics.
- End with the smallest operational decision, its owner, verification, and stop rule.

When an audience needs a short message, include current condition, decisive evidence, proposed actions, and the decision needed. Respect the requested capitalization and channel syntax. Include sensitive identifiers only when authorized and necessary for execution.
