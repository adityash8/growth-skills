# Dated unit-economics benchmark context

Source-library verification date: July 2026; underlying reports: 2025. This public adaptation has not independently reverified the figures. Recheck after twelve months or before relying on them for a material decision. Business-specific, approved thresholds and available capital take priority.

## Sources

- Benchmarkit, 2025 SaaS Performance Metrics Benchmarks: https://www.benchmarkit.ai/2025benchmarks
- Fiscallion, SaaS Unit Economics: https://www.fiscallion.io/blog/saas-unit-economics

Label cited figures [Benchmark: source/year]. Match segment, acquisition-cost loading, margin basis, billing terms and period. These are reference thresholds, not universal guarantees.

## Reference bands

| Metric | Dated context |
|---|---|
| LTV:CAC below 1 | Lifetime gross profit does not recover acquisition cost under the model |
| LTV:CAC 1–2 | Weak economics; repair before increasing spend |
| LTV:CAC approximately 3.2–3.6 | Reported median, not evidence of strong performance |
| LTV:CAC 4–5 | Stronger reference range; potential Accelerate only with other checks passing |
| LTV:CAC above 5 | Strong modeled economics or possible underinvestment; investigate assumptions |
| Blended gross-margin CAC payback | Reported median approximately 20 months; an average is not a pass |

## Provisional payback thresholds carried from the source

| Motion | Reference threshold for Accelerate |
|---|---|
| Small-business / self-serve / monthly | Below 12 months |
| Mid-market | Below 18 months |
| Enterprise / prepaid annual or longer | Below 24 months |

Apply to fully loaded, gross-margin-adjusted CAC payback for SaaS. Use contribution-margin payback for transactional, marketplace and direct-to-consumer subscriptions; use the monthly threshold unless contract and cash evidence supports another choice. State the chosen threshold and why it fits. Short cash reserves may require faster repayment.

Net revenue churn above 2% monthly is a historical structural-risk heuristic carried from the source library, not a verified current market benchmark. Expansion-led models need NRR above 100% to expand the opening revenue base. Always show logo counts alongside NRR and explain maturity and expansion assumptions.

Do not use old claims of a universal 5–12 month average payback, or a universal 3:1 LTV:CAC success threshold, as current verdict rules. Dated consumer payback polls and churn bands are context, not decision thresholds. Missing current evidence means provisional grading, not invented verification.
